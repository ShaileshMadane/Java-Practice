🎰 Slot Machine — Java

A simple console-based Slot Machine game built in Java. The player starts with a balance of $100, places bets, spins three random symbols, and receives payouts based on the matching symbols.

📌 Features
💰 Starting balance of $100
🎯 User-defined bet amount
🎰 Random slot spinning using Java's Random class
🍒 Multiple slot symbols:
🍒 Cherry
🍉 Watermelon
🍋 Lemon
🔔 Bell
⭐ Star
🏆 Different payouts for different symbols
💵 Partial-match payouts
❌ Validation for invalid and insufficient bets
🔄 Option to play multiple rounds
🖥️ Simple console-based interface
🎮 How the Game Works
The game starts with a balance of $100.
The player enters a bet amount.
The bet is deducted from the current balance.
Three random symbols are generated.
The symbols are displayed on the screen.
The payout is calculated based on the result.
If the player wins, the payout is added back to the balance.
The player can choose whether to play another round.
The game ends when:
The player chooses N, or
The balance reaches zero.
💰 Payout System
Three Matching Symbols
Symbol	Payout
🍒	Bet × 4
🍉	Bet × 5
🍋	Bet × 6
🔔	Bet × 7
⭐	Bet × 8
Two Matching Symbols
Symbol	Payout
🍒	Bet × 2
🍉	Bet × 3
🍋	Bet × 4
🔔	Bet × 5
⭐	Bet × 6

If there are no matching symbols, the player receives no payout.
